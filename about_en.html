Cheng-Hong Li

Li Cheng-Hong, Levi

Cheng-Hong Li is a researcher who graduated from the Genetics and Breeding program of the Department of Agronomy at National Chung Hsing University, specializing in genetics and rice quality.

Research projects undertaken include: Ministry of Agriculture research projects
Breeding research to improve disease resistance in japonica rice in the Taitung region
Research on key rice cultivation techniques in central Taiwan
Research on climate-adaptive rice cultivation techniques in central Taiwan
Mitigation of high-temperature impacts on rice quality through fertilizer management in central Taiwan
Development and application of high-throughput rice quality analysis techniques

Expertise and Professional Activities

  • Rice cultivation and breeding, rice quality, molecular cytogenetics, and statistical programming
  • Judge, Rice Master Competition
  • Member and certified Rice Taste Appraiser
  • Rice grain inspection training, Agriculture and Food Agency
  • Lecturer on rice quality and sensory evaluation (National Chung Hsing University, News & Market, Big Rice Pot, Hupu Community College, farmers' associations, and rice mills)

Education and Professional Experience

  • B.S., Department of Agronomy, National Chung Hsing University (2012-2016)
  • M.S., Graduate Institute of Agronomy, National Chung Hsing University Thesis (2016-2019)
  • Research Assistant, Rice Research Laboratory, Crop Science Division, Taiwan Agricultural Research Institute, Council of Agriculture, Executive Yuan (2019-2020)
  • Acting Research Staff, Upland Crop Research Laboratory, Crop Science Division, Taiwan Agricultural Research Institute, Council of Agriculture, Executive Yuan (2020)
  • Assistant Researcher, Taitung District Agricultural Research and Extension Station, Council of Agriculture, Executive Yuan (2020-2022)
  • Assistant Researcher, Taichung District Agricultural Research and Extension Station, Council of Agriculture, Executive Yuan (2022-July 2023)
  • Assistant Researcher, Taichung District Agricultural Research and Extension Station, Ministry of Agriculture (August 2023-present)
