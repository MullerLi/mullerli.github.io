Cheng-Hong Li

Li Cheng-Hong, Levi

Cheng-Hong Li is an agricultural researcher specializing in genetics, rice cultivation, and rice quality. His research focuses on climate adaptation in rice production, rice quality evaluation, crop genetics, and agricultural data analysis.
